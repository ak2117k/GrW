## Caveats

- Single market regime: the legacy data covers about two months of one market phase (see the data window); the VIX-regime slices show how little regime variety there is.
- Long-only cash equity in practice (gated SELL entries are about 1%); nothing here speaks for options, futures or shorts.
- Labels are fee-dominated: small gross moves on paper position sizes mean fees often turn a gross win into a net loss, so `win` is partly a fee-hurdle label.
- Derived R: most legacy trades stored no stop, so R uses the legacy loss-cut (flat Rs 1,000 before 2026-05-19, 0.4% of deployed capital after) and is marked rDerived.
- The three legacy tracks traded the same Chartink alerts; setups are de-duplicated by alert|symbol|IST date before the split, so one alert counts once (the earliest track's record is kept).

  into the gated/ungated records of the same alerts (211 rows before, 19 after).
- Features, thresholds and the gate were not tuned after seeing these results.
